# Grok Wealth Protocol v2 — Family Operating System
**Effective:** 2026-09-08  
**Owner:** Family of 4, Cleveland / White County, Georgia  
**North star:** Comfortable middle class ($100k+ household run-rate), security, health, time for memory-making, shared work, and travel that serves the work.

This document replaces the June 2026 “God Protocol / $102 trillion in 60 minutes” framing. That version was a creative sprint. It is not an operating system. v2 is.

## Hard constraints (non-negotiable)
1. **SSI / Medicaid / SNAP protection first.** Any income path must be PASS-compatible or explicitly below countable-income tripwires until PASS is approved. Do not treat simulated ledger totals as cash.
2. **Low-physical / voice / laptop first.** Neck and right-arm limitation is a design constraint, not a footnote.
3. **Truth ledger only.** Track only money that actually moved or a signed application that is pending. No “oracle” balances.
4. **Family time is a KPI**, not leftover. Calendar memory blocks before work blocks.
5. **No high-risk trading as the primary engine.** Paper desks and prediction kits stay archived. Live size only after emergency fund + PASS + advisor.

## What v1 got right
- Family is the unit of account, not the founder ego.
- Unclaimed property, 529s, revocable trust, term life, and beneficiary updates are still high-ROI, low-glamour work.
- Automation for the spouse (few commands, one dashboard) is the correct UX.
- Digital products can be a stream. They are not a lottery ticket.

## What v1 got wrong
- Claimed $33k+ “ledger” without bank confirmation.
- Sold velocity theater (4-hour god loops, pantheon of sub-gods, $102T) instead of a 90-day cash plan.
- Tiny stub files (`wealth_ledger.py` ~79 bytes, `protocol.html` 47 bytes) presented as tools.
- Gumroad “Family Wealth Codex” as the main engine before a real product people would pay for existed.
- No SSI/PASS gate on every revenue idea.
- No Plaid / real books.
- No research-participation path for UBI/UHI despite that being the only public “universal high income” conversation that exists in 2026.

## Operating model — one company, four roles
Work is something the household points at together. Not everyone codes.

- **Architect:** laptop / voice. Grants, PASS, agents, music systems, research liaison.
- **Operations (spouse):** payouts, calendar, 529 contributions, claim follow-up, “is this week a memory week?” veto.
- **Producer (teen):** catalog metadata, clips, school-compatible merch/design, paid only inside PASS rules.
- **Witness (younger):** memory capture (photos, voice notes), “what did we do that was good?” weekly review.

Travel rule: a trip is approved if it is either (a) a memory block with zero work obligation or (b) work that funds itself and includes the family without wrecking school.

## Income stack (priority order)
1. **Protect the floor** — SSI + Medicaid + SNAP + PASS.
2. **Lump sums that are not earnings** — unclaimed property, tax credits, insurance, inheritances, certain grant draws structured correctly.
3. **Non-dilutive Georgia capital** — GVRA, OneGeorgia / rural development, SBDC, DOBE, education awards. Digital/non-phone preferred.
4. **PASS-housed self-employment** — one business, written plan, countable income parked toward the goal (equipment, vehicle that reduces physical load, education, inventory).
5. **Catalog & IP** — existing music + visuals as a *system*, not a new identity every week.
6. **Research participation / field-site admin** — UBI/UHI/GI studies, Anthropic Economic Futures *via an institution*, university partnership, rural Georgia GI expansion.
7. **Marketplace / agent fees** — only after 1–4 are running.

Cut until this list is short.

## UHI / xAI / SpaceX reality check
There is **no standing public protocol** at xAI, SpaceX, or Grok that enrolls a typical family of 4 into Universal High Income, family-management stipends, or “early beta UHI.” Musk’s UHI comments are a future-policy thesis (AI abundance → high income), not an application portal.

What *does* exist in 2026 and can be used:

- Municipal / nonprofit **guaranteed income** pilots (Atlanta I.M.P.A.C.T. $500/mo × 12 for city residents under ~200% FPL; Georgia “In Her Hands” completed phases; GRO / Freedom Futures rural Georgia *youth* cohort fundraising for 2026). White County residency generally **does not** qualify for Atlanta city pilots.
- Academic and philanthropic RCTs (OpenResearch / ORUS-style $1,000/mo studies; Stanford Basic Income Lab dashboard; Mayors for a Guaranteed Income).
- Anthropic **Economic Futures Research Fund** ($200M) — funds *institutions* for longer unconditional-income pilots including family stability and child-development outcomes. Individuals cannot apply solo. Path = partner with UGA / App State / Washington University / a Georgia nonprofit as field family + rural site.
- Researcher / enumerator / community-liaison paid roles on those studies (often $20–40/hr, travel, sometimes family-inclusive fieldwork). This is the realistic “admin researcher” door.
