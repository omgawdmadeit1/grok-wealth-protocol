# UBI / UHI research map — September 2026
For a typical U.S. family of 4 that wants to **participate, administer locally, or help stand up a study** — not wait for a federal check that does not exist.

## Definitions (do not mix them)
- **UBI** — universal, unconditional, periodic cash. National UBI is not law in the United States.
- **Guaranteed income (GI)** — targeted, time-limited, usually city/philanthropy funded. This is what actually pays people in 2026.
- **UHI (Universal High Income)** — Elon Musk / post-scarcity framing: not a floor of $1k/mo but rising prosperity as AI + robots collapse the cost of goods. **There is no enrollment form.** It is a thesis, not a program.

## What the evidence actually says
Across 100+ U.S. municipal pilots and several large RCTs:

- Cash reduces stress, improves sleep and reported parenting, and increases spending on children. Long-run Cherokee casino dividend work (JAMA Pediatrics) associated childhood cash with better adult mental health and functioning.
- OpenResearch Unconditional Income Study (ORUS): $1,000/mo × 3 years vs $50/mo control. Parents spent more on kids and reported closer supervision. Employment effects are modest declines on average (a few percentage points / 1–2 hours per week in several evaluations), larger for higher-income and childless adults.
- Georgia *In Her Hands* (Black women, ~$850/mo, 24 months, Atlanta + College Park + SW Georgia rural cluster): mixed labor results; some waves showed increased paid hours and reduced caregiving hours among mothers; stress/sleep improved.
- Atlanta I.M.P.A.C.T.: $500/mo × 12, city residents under ~200% FPL (~$53k for a family of 4). New 2026 citywide wave: 300 seats, Urban League of Greater Atlanta + Mayors for a Guaranteed Income. **Cleveland / White County residents are out of catchment.**
- GRO / Freedom Futures (Georgia): youth (18–25) GI + $40k baby bond; rural Georgia cohort planned for 2026 pending ~$6M raise. Wrong age band for the parents; possible future door for a teen only if eligibility matches.

Cash is not a career. It is a stabilizer. That is still valuable.

### B. Get paid to help run the research (higher EV than waiting)

### C. Stand up a study and seek funding
You cannot apply to Anthropic’s Economic Futures Research Fund as a private individual. They fund universities, research institutes, and nonprofits with field-experiment track records. Fundable theme that matches this family:

> Longer-duration unconditional income at livable levels, with outcomes on wellbeing, family stability, child development, civic participation, and how recipients structure their time — plus rural / disability / SSI-interaction, which is under-studied.

Build sequence (12–20 weeks):
1. One-page protocol: rural north-Georgia family-of-4 + disability-benefits interaction + time-use / memory / health KPIs.
2. Find a PI who already evaluates GI. Offer to be the rural community partner, not the scientist.
3. Host institution submits to Anthropic Economic Futures, RWJF, Arnold Ventures, MGI research RFPs, or DBHDD innovation dollars if framed as disability + family stability.
4. If funded, household can be a pilot family and/or paid community researchers only if the IRB allows disclosure.

### D. Georgia programs that are not UBI but move the same needle
SSI + PASS, GVRA, OneGeorgia / rural development, SNAP / Medicaid, 529 + education awards.

A GI check can still be countable for SSI. Never enroll in a cash pilot without a benefits counselor.
