**Family operating system — not a conquest myth.**

## What this is
A reorganized, truth-first protocol for one family of four in rural north Georgia to reach **comfortable middle-class security ($100k+ household run-rate as the design target)**, protect health and benefits, keep time for memories, and treat work as something the household points at together — including travel that is either pure memory or self-funding fieldwork.

Created June 16, 2026 as a “God Protocol” sprint. Reorganized September 8, 2026 after a full review of the repo, the intervening agent/Plaid/x402/local-build stack, and the actual 2026 UBI/UHI research landscape.
